Privacy Notice

This is a demonstration website. Submissions are stored only to show how an application record is created.

  • We store your name, mobile number, requested amount and currency, consent version and submission time.
  • Only the last four digits of the national ID are stored. The full number is never saved.
  • No data is shared with third parties, credit bureaus or lenders.
  • Test data may be deleted at any time.

KVKK Information

Under Turkey's Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK), a data controller must explain the purpose, legal basis and recipients of processing, and the rights of data subjects.

For this demo the purpose is solely to demonstrate an application flow, the legal basis is your explicit consent, and there are no recipients. You can ask for access, correction or deletion. In a real deployment, the operator's controller details would appear here.

UK GDPR

Under the UK GDPR you have rights to access, rectify, erase, restrict and object to processing of your personal data, and to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

This demonstration processes data on the basis of consent, which you may withdraw at any time. A real operator would list its identity, contact details and retention periods here.
